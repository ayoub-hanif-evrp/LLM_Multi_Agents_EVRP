# Recovery decision

Baseline commit: `dd5949d0a5a7f679f3ba14a411539a23110c0d0a`.

## Why direct route generation is no longer the primary track

The frozen classical backbone is feasible on all 39 development instances. A deterministic customer pack plus labeled energy repair is feasible on all four small pilot instances with zero model calls. The latest direct-agent recovery is feasible on 1 of 4 instances after 20 model calls, and the one feasible Bezzi cost, 225, equals the deterministic repair cost. Earlier LLM scoring changes did not survive confirmation.

Free-form route generation therefore stays in the repository as the exploratory `agentic_v2` track. It is not deleted. It is not the experiment that this recovery spends model calls on.

## Hypothesis

Unproven. The question is whether specialized model roles can produce an executable insertion, station, or removal policy that changes a real decision of the frozen classical solver and then improves its native objective under a shared budget. A policy that only changes a number, or a feasible route produced by the classical repair, is not evidence for that hypothesis.

## What stays frozen

`ProblemSpecV2`, `evaluate_solution_v2`, the native objectives, construction, ALNS, charging dynamic programming, energy repair, dataset hashes, and the historical result directories are unchanged. A result that uses the charging DP or energy repair keeps that method name. It is not labeled as an LLM discovery.
